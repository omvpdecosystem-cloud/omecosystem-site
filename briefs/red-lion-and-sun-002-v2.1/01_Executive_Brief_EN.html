What must a humanitarian ecosystem be able to do when crisis arrives?

From 1922 to 1980 Iran's national relief society served under a red lion and sun. This brief summarises a research and design proposal for how that humanitarian function could be redesigned as a modern, independent, citizen-centred ecosystem able to act in the crises of the present: protection of civilians, continuity of care, communication and essential needs, and documentation under strict evidence discipline. What is proposed does not exist; what is contested is marked contested; what is documented carries its class.

Documented present Conditions at evidence discipline Water stress, power outages, medicine shortages, food inflation ≈58% (Sep 2025, WFP figure), and since Feb 2026 a nationwide complex emergency disrupting services for millions. Documented patterns Harm where protection was missing Civilian harm in protest crackdowns; facility intrusion and detention of the wounded; recurring shutdowns — incl. the longest nationwide blackout recorded, from 8 Jan 2026. Central proposition Protection as a design objective Five new service domains: civilian protection & early warning · communication continuity · essential-needs continuity · medical protection · consent-based documentation.
OM Ecosystem approach Five nested layers Policy · System · Organization · Technology · Service — one centre: the person served. Every risk processed through the Human Harm Prevention Matrix; every technology paired with its risk. Evidence discipline No silent upgrades Verified fact · credible report · attributed claim · unverified. Conflicts and gaps held open: casualty totals differ by source; cross-border armed-actor claims remain unverified. Standing boundaries Humanitarian only No tactics, no political alignment, no enforcement role; border security stays with competent authorities. The future ecosystem is a design proposal — not an organisation that exists.
The distinction that governs the package

Historical record — what the Society was and did.  Analytical assessment — documented conditions and harms at registered evidence classes, and what they imply.  Future design proposal — a civilian-protection ecosystem, proposed and never claimed to exist.
