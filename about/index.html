About the OM Ecosystem

The OM Ecosystem has three tiers: a foundation, a shared platform and three pillars.

  • FoundationOnion Theory · LEGA — the theoretical and architectural basis.
  • Shared software platformOM-VPD — the shared software and computational decision-support infrastructure. It is not a pillar.
  • PillarsOM-R&D · OM-LOS · OM-AI — the three organisational and operational domains.

What each name means

Onion TheoryFOUNDATION
The theory: decisions in a socio-technical ecosystem are made across five governance layers — Product, Technology, Organization, System and Policy — and their effects propagate between those layers.
Onion ModelFOUNDATION
The framework that expresses the theory: five nested layers with one shared tangent point, used to coordinate decisions across the layers and trace how they propagate.
LEGAFOUNDATION
The Layered Ecosystem Governance Architecture: the architectural representation of the theory, which says how a system that embodies it must be structured.
OM-VPDSHARED SOFTWARE PLATFORM
The Onion Model – Value Propagation Decision Platform: the decision-support software that implements aspects of Onion Theory following LEGA. It represents an ecosystem as decision nodes across the five layers, propagates the effect of changes and compares decision options. It is shared by the pillars and is not one of them.
OM-R&DPILLAR
Research & Development Services. Research, publication and demonstration.
OM-LOSPILLAR
Leading, Offering & Services. Providing commercial products and services based on OM-R&D research and development.
OM-AIPILLAR
AI Platform & Services. Providing AI services informed by Onion Model thinking and perspectives. It will provide tools for defining, generating, and representing input packages for the software, and for interpreting or post-processing its outputs.

The governance architecture — not the number of websites or domains — defines the system.

Application-domain independence

The scientific identity of the Onion Theory, LEGA, and the OM-VPD Platform is independent of any individual application domain. Application domains serve as certified reference ecosystems through which the general theory is demonstrated. Domain-specific methodologies (e.g., LCA, LCM, BIM, Digital Twins, optimization, simulation, or AI techniques) are treated as contextual decision processes or enabling methods within an ecosystem rather than as defining characteristics of the theory or platform.

OM-R&D

Research Pillar

Research identity is independent of the domain it is demonstrated in. Certified reference ecosystems are where the general theory is shown to hold; they are evidence for it, never a definition of it. A methodology used inside one — life-cycle assessment, simulation, optimisation — is a decision process applied within an ecosystem, not a characteristic of OM-R&D.

OM-LOS

Commercial Technology Platform

Commercial identity is independent of any single engagement or sector. The services, offerings and delivery workflows OM-LOS runs are contextual implementations chosen for a client's situation; they are what the pillar does in one case, not what the pillar is. A domain methodology carried into an engagement stays a method within it.

OM-AI

Intelligent Interaction Platform

Platform identity is independent of the techniques it happens to use. Models, architectures, agent designs and application contexts are enabling capabilities applied within ecosystems; replacing any of them leaves OM-AI what it was. The pillar is the governed interaction surface, not the current generation of methods behind it.

Within OM-R&D, certified reference ecosystems demonstrate the theory — first among them Residential Buildings — and methods like life-cycle management or digital twins are contextual decision processes, never the identity.

Scientific–commercial separation

Scientific governance (Onion Theory and OM-R&D) and commercial governance (OM-LOS) remain organizationally and conceptually distinct. Commercial products may be informed by validated research, but commercial objectives never redefine the scientific theory, alter certified evidence, or override OM-R&D’s scientific governance.

Where application domains sit

Everything below is applied within an ecosystem. A domain and a method are contexts the pillars work in — neither is a pillar, an identity, or an architectural layer of the ecosystem.

OM Ecosystem

The Onion Model is a layered framework for Ecosystem Management and Ecosystem Governance that enables the coordination, propagation, validation, and explanation of decisions across complex socio-technical ecosystems. OM-R&D is the research pillar of the OM Ecosystem. OM-VPD is the shared software platform of the OM Ecosystem.
