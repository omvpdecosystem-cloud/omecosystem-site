About OM AI

OM AI is the Intelligent Interaction Platform of the OM Ecosystem.

OM AI is the Intelligent Interaction Platform of the OM Ecosystem — the surface where humans, agents and knowledge meet under shared governance.

The three pillars

The OM Ecosystem is built on three constitutional pillars. Each has its own identity class and mission. They are peers, not hierarchies.

PillarIdentity ClassMission
OM-VPD Scientific Research Platform Research, validation, governance, publications, datasets, evidence
AI-LOS Commercial Technology Platform Products, services, commercialization, enterprise solutions
OM AI Intelligent Interaction Platform Human–AI collaboration, intelligent agents, workspaces, knowledge interaction

What OM AI is not

General assistants optimise fluency. OM AI optimises ecosystem position, dependency awareness and governance consequence.

  • A product. Products are AI-LOS territory. OM AI is the interaction layer products are built on.
  • A chatbot. Conversation is an interface, never the identity. Reasoning precedes response.
  • An LLM wrapper. Providers are swappable. The Onion reasoning pipeline is the constant.

Ecosystem-serving. One intelligent interaction platform serving all three pillars equally.

Commercial and governance separation

OM AI itself is an architectural pillar and is never commercial. Software built within this pillar may be licensed through AI-LOS under separate commercial governance, but that does not change OM AI’s constitutional role.

The OM AI pillar is public and user-facing, while its underlying intelligence infrastructure remains separate and is not exposed as part of this website section.

Naming and endorsement

"OM AI" is set with a single space, both words capitalised. No hyphen, no lowercase "ai", no possessive forms.

When two pillars appear together, marks are set at equal optical weight, alphabetical by designation, separated by a hairline — never nested.

The approved attribution is “Reasoning by OM AI”. OM AI may be credited as "Reasoning by OM AI" inside AI-LOS products and OM-VPD workflows; it never carries the host product’s name.
