REFERENCE CANDIDATE

Agriculture Ecosystem

Governing decisions across farms, supply networks, technology adoption, and agricultural policy.

Agriculture is a candidate reference ecosystem: multi-stakeholder, policy-shaped, technology-mediated, and rich in decision propagation between field-level products and national policy. A certification program here would extend the body of evidence without changing the theory’s identity.

The five layers in this ecosystem

LayerDefinition
Policy L5 · OUTERWhere regulation, incentives, and public governance shape the ecosystem.
System L4Where infrastructures, markets, and networks interconnect.
Organization L3Where institutions coordinate stakeholders and resources.
Technology L2Where platforms, data, and digital twins operate.
Product L1 · COREWhere decisions materialize into products, services, and outcomes.

CONTEXTUAL DECISION PROCESSES (ENABLING METHODS — NOT THE IDENTITY): TECHNOLOGY ADOPTION · SUSTAINABILITY ASSESSMENT · RISK MANAGEMENT

OM-VPD — Onion Model Validation, Publication, and Demonstration Platform

The Onion Model is a layered framework for Ecosystem Management and Ecosystem Governance that enables the coordination, propagation, validation, and explanation of decisions across complex socio-technical ecosystems. OM-VPD is the validated research platform that operationalizes this framework and provides reproducible evidence, demonstrations, and tools for ecosystem-oriented decision intelligence.
