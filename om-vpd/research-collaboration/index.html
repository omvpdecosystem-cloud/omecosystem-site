Are you researching a system that never stops changing?

Technologies evolve. Institutions adapt. Policies shift. Organizations respond.
A decision taken at one level rarely stays there; its effects travel across the system.

If you are Researcher and your research examines decisions within complex, evolving environments. If you are Principal Investigator and your research involves interactions across multiple levels of a system. If you are PhD Researcher and you are looking for a structured way to examine relationships between decisions, actors, technologies, and their wider environment.
If you are Research Group and your existing research context could provide a meaningful testbed for a new analytical framework. If you are Interdisciplinary Research Team and your research crosses organizational, technological, institutional, or policy boundaries. If you are University Research or Innovation Center and you are interested in exploring and critically evaluating new approaches to complex-system analysis.
The idea

Research questions do not exist in isolation. They emerge where layers interact and decisions propagate. OM-VPD offers a structured, domain-independent framework for examining these relationships.

OM-VPD — Onion Model Validation, Publication, and Demonstration Platform

The Onion Model is a layered framework for Ecosystem Management and Ecosystem Governance that enables the coordination, propagation, validation, and explanation of decisions across complex socio-technical ecosystems. OM-VPD is the validated research platform that operationalizes this framework and provides reproducible evidence, demonstrations, and tools for ecosystem-oriented decision intelligence.
