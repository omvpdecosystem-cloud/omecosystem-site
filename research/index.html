RESEARCH PROGRAM

Research

The OM-VPD research program develops and demonstrates the Onion Theory across certified reference ecosystems, with reproducibility as a first-class requirement: runs and seeds are recorded, outputs are traceable, and results become citable records.

Streams

Theory — the Onion Theory and LEGA. Application — demonstration within certified reference ecosystems, beginning with Residential Buildings. Computation — the OM-VPD Platform implementation. The application paper demonstrates the theory within a certified reference ecosystem; the theory itself is validated through the broader validation program.

OM-VPD — Onion Model Validation, Publication, and Demonstration Platform

The Onion Model is a layered framework for Ecosystem Management and Ecosystem Governance that enables the coordination, propagation, validation, and explanation of decisions across complex socio-technical ecosystems. OM-VPD is the validated research platform that operationalizes this framework and provides reproducible evidence, demonstrations, and tools for ecosystem-oriented decision intelligence.
