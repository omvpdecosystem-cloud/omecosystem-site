AL-11 · THE DECISION LOOP

We frame every engagement as a governed decision loop: context and data inform a model, the model’s reasoning is made explainable and decided on, action is taken, and feedback returns to sharpen the next decision.

Governance wraps the whole loop — nothing acts outside it.

Grounded in research

SEPARATION
Scientific governance (Onion Theory and OM-VPD) and commercial governance (OM-LOS) remain organizationally and conceptually distinct. Commercial products may be informed by validated research, but commercial objectives never redefine the scientific theory, alter certified evidence, or override OM-VPD’s scientific governance.

Every commercial capability traces to the science where applicable: the Onion Theory supplies the governance model, OM-VPD supplies certified evidence, and OM-LOS engineers and ships it. Scientific evidence is never altered for commercial purposes.
