What is OM Ecosystem?

A single system for governing complex decisions. One scientific foundation — the Onion Model — carried into practice by three pillars: research that validates it, services that apply it, and AI that makes it usable. Each pillar stands on its own; together they are one ecosystem.

OFFICIAL POSITIONING STATEMENT

The Onion Model is a layered framework for Ecosystem Management and Ecosystem Governance that enables the coordination, propagation, validation, and explanation of decisions across complex socio-technical ecosystems.

OM-VPD is the validated research platform that operationalizes this framework and provides reproducible evidence, demonstrations, and tools for ecosystem-oriented decision intelligence.
