GOVERNANCE

Governance

Research is only as useful as it is trustworthy. These are the commitments that hold across everything published here — they constrain what the platform may do to its own evidence, and they do not change because a result becomes commercially inconvenient.

Evidence is never altered after certification

Certified results are fixed. A finding that turns out to be unwelcome is superseded by new work and a new version — never edited in place, never quietly withdrawn. What was cited stays citable.

Scientific and commercial governance stay separate

Scientific governance (Onion Theory and OM-VPD) and commercial governance (AI-LOS) remain organizationally and conceptually distinct. Commercial products may be informed by validated research, but commercial objectives never redefine the scientific theory, alter certified evidence, or override OM-VPD’s scientific governance.

The theory is independent of any single application domain

The scientific identity of the Onion Theory, LEGA, and the OM-VPD Platform is independent of any individual application domain. Application domains serve as certified reference ecosystems through which the general theory is demonstrated. Domain-specific methodologies (e.g., LCA, LCM, BIM, Digital Twins, optimization, simulation, or AI techniques) are treated as contextual decision processes or enabling methods within an ecosystem rather than as defining characteristics of the theory or platform.

Published work keeps its address

URLs for publications, datasets, and reference ecosystems are treated as citations, not as navigation. They are not reorganised for convenience, and a superseded version remains reachable at the address it was cited under.

See Validation for how evidence is produced and checked, and Reference Ecosystems for how the theory is demonstrated in practice.
